BOUGAINVILLE IMPACT

Ownership should create capability — not just a share certificate.

BIGL's long-term success will be measured by commercial performance and by the practical capability it helps build in Bougainville.

Local EmploymentGrow Bougainvillean participation across administration, procurement, sales, warehousing and technical roles.
Structured TrainingSupplier training, systems development, technical familiarisation and supervised skill progression.
Graduate PathwaysFuture opportunities for engineering, science, logistics, business and technology graduates.
Trades DevelopmentApprenticeships and traineeships when field-service and workshop volumes support them.
Local Supplier DevelopmentHelp capable Bougainvillean transport, fabrication, maintenance and service providers raise standards.
Leadership SuccessionDevelop a pipeline for supervisory, management and board-level commercial leadership.
Investor ParticipationSubject to proper legal structuring, enable eligible Bougainvilleans to participate in ownership.
Economic Value RetentionKeep a greater share of industrial margins, expertise, employment and future dividends within Bougainville.
