OWNERSHIP & GOVERNANCE

Local ownership with commercial independence.

BIGL is envisioned as a Bougainville-owned commercial enterprise with governance capable of supporting public and private shareholder participation.

PROPOSED FRAMEWORK

Ownership percentages are not fixed yet.

The final structure should follow a bottom-up capital model, defendable valuation, legal advice and formal shareholder agreements.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

Professional management is essential.

Government participation should strengthen legitimacy and development alignment without converting BIGL into a government department.

  • Qualified and appropriately representative board
  • Day-to-day authority delegated to professional management
  • Reserved matters for major transactions
  • Annual external audit
  • Formal procurement and delegation policies
  • Conflict-of-interest and related-party controls
  • Transparent recruitment and performance management
  • Disciplined dividend and working-capital policy
