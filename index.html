OUR PURPOSE

Keep more industrial value in Bougainville.

BIGL is being developed to create local ownership, employment, skills and commercial capability while delivering dependable industrial solutions to customers.

Proposed PNG-registered company • Bougainville-focused • Commercially managed

WHO WE ARE

A new industrial platform for Bougainville.

BIGL is being established as a commercially focused industrial supply and technical-services company serving Bougainville and the wider PNG market.

Our goal is to connect customers with quality equipment, spare parts, engineering support, technology and specialist expertise while building meaningful local capability and investment participation.
