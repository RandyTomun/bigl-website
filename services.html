PRODUCTS & SERVICES

Industrial Supply

Pumps, valves, piping, hoses, fittings, bearings, seals, fasteners, tools, workshop consumables, PPE and MRO items.

Mining & Process

Process-plant components, screens, cyclones, wear parts, sampling equipment and selected mobile-equipment spares.

Laboratory & Technical

Sample-preparation equipment, balances, furnaces, laboratory consumables, glassware and QA/QC supplies.

Electrical & Instrumentation

Motors, drives, sensors, transmitters, controls, cables, switchgear and instrumentation spares.

Engineering & Field Services

Installation support, inspections, troubleshooting, shutdown support and commissioning coordination.

Technology

Industrial hardware and software, communications, monitoring and selected automation products.

Procurement & Sourcing

RFQ management, difficult-to-source parts, supplier identification, consolidation, documentation and expediting.

Training & Workforce Development

Product training, procurement systems, warehousing, technical familiarisation and future trade/graduate pathways.
