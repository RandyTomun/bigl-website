ABOUT BIGL

A Bougainville industrial company built for the long term.

BIGL is proposed as a PNG-registered, Bougainville-focused commercial enterprise supplying equipment, engineering support, technology and technical services.

STRATEGIC PURPOSE

Capture more value locally.

As Bougainville develops, customers will require increasing volumes of equipment, parts, consumables, technology and specialist support.

BIGL is designed to compete for that work while building a stronger local commercial and technical base.

VISION

To become Bougainville's leading locally owned industrial supply and technical-services group.

MISSION

Connect industry with quality equipment, technology and expertise while building skills, jobs and ownership.

OUR VALUES

How BIGL intends to operate.

Integrity

Transparent procurement, accurate reporting and strong conflict-of-interest controls.

Safety

Safe products, competent execution and responsible field practices.

Capability

Invest in people, systems and technical knowledge.

Commercial Discipline

Protect cash flow, price responsibly and pursue sustainable margins.

Local Participation

Meaningful Bougainvillean ownership, employment and supplier development.

Partnership

Constructive relationships with customers, government, communities, OEMs and service partners.
